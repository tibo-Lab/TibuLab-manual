TibuLab User Manual

Step-by-step help, with pictures of the real screens, for everyone in the lab: reception, sample collection, technicians, pathologists, radiology, cashiers, accounts and HR. The pictures come from a demo lab with made-up patients.

Pick your job to jump to your part:

Receptionist / CashierRegister, New Bill, payments, returns Sample CollectorCollect samples, print labels Lab TechnicianEnter results, report entry PathologistVerify, critical results, share RadiologyWorklist, scan, reporting AccountantCash book, ledger, statements HREmployees, attendance, payroll Owner / AdminSetup, users, settings
Tip: you only see the menus your role and your lab's plan allow. If a screen in this manual is missing for you, ask your lab owner. Click any picture to open it full size.

Login and the top bar

Your lab's address, e.g. citylab.tibulab.com
  1. Open your lab's address in Chrome or Edge. If you only know your lab code (like TL001), open tibulab.com, click Login, type the code and click Continue.
  2. Type your Email and Password. Leave Keep me signed in ticked on your own PC, and click Sign in.
  3. "Email or password is incorrect." means a typing mistake. After many wrong tries you must wait a minute.

Dashboard

Dashboard

Today at a glance: Patients today, Sales today, Collected today and Pending dues. Work status counts samples waiting at each step (collection, result entry, verification, critical results) and radiology work; click a line to open that list. Recent bills shows today's bills, and New Bill opens the POS.

Open register→New Bill→Collect samples→Enter results→Verify→Print / share→Close register→Day closing

Open the cash register (start of every shift)

Reception › New Bill

Money is only taken on an open register. When you open New Bill and your register is closed, the Open Cash Register box appears by itself.

  1. Choose your Counter.
  2. Enter the Cash in hand: the cash already in the drawer.
  3. Click Open register. The top of the POS turns green with your counter name and the time it opened.
"No counter is set up for you" means the admin must first add a counter in Billing › Cash Registers.

New Bill (POS): register and bill a patient

Reception › New Bill

One bill can hold lab tests, radiology exams and services. The POS opens full screen.

1. Find or add the patient

  1. Type in Enter name / phone / MRN. A full mobile number lists everyone who uses that number, so family members appear together. Pick the right person.
  2. Not found? Click the blue + (New patient) and fill in Title, Name, Gender, Age, Phone, CNIC and City, then save.
  3. Adding a family member on someone else's number asks for the Relation to owner.

2. Doctor, sample source, tests

  1. Ref by (doctor): the doctor who sent the patient (this is how commission is counted).
  2. Sample source: Taking in lab, Home collection, Sample from outside, OPD, IPD / Ward, Emergency, Referred lab.
  3. Press F2 or click Search test (F2) and type 2+ letters. One match is added at once; otherwise pick from the list and press Enter. Radiology exams and services come from the same box. The same test can't be added twice.
  4. Remove a line with the red ×.

3. Insurance and discount

  • Paid by: choose the insurer (panel / Sehat Card) and type the card number. The screen shows Insurer pays and Patient pays.
  • Discount (-): click the pencil, enter the amount and a reason. Each role has a limit (Receptionist 10%, Manager 25%).
  • An Old due bar appears if the patient owes from earlier bills; Pay due takes that money.

Taking payment

Bottom bar of the POS
Button (key)What happens
Cash (F8)Whole amount in cash. Saved and printed at once, no questions.
Card (F9)Card details box: card no., holder name, transaction no. → Finalize Payment. Only the last 4 digits are kept.
Multiple Pay (F10)Split between Cash, Card, JazzCash, Easypaisa, Bank transfer, Cheque. Add Payment Row for each, then Finalize Payment.

After saving you see Bill saved …, the invoice prints by itself, and the screen clears for the next patient. Tube labels are printed later on the Lab screen.

"Select or add the patient first" or "Add at least one test" means something is missing. If the register is closed, the Open register box appears instead of taking money.

Recent bills, sell return, register details, expense

POS toolbar (top right) and bottom bar

Reprint, payments, cancel

Recent Transactions lists bills with Invoice, Patient, Time, Total, Due and Status. Each row has Print (reprint), View payments, Add payment (while money is due) and Cancel bill.

Sell return (refund at the counter)

  1. Click the red ↺ Sell return icon, type the Invoice No. and click Send.
  2. The Cancel bill box opens for that bill. Type the Reason for cancelling. It shows the Money to give back; choose how (Cash comes from your open register).
  3. Click Cancel bill and confirm. The lab and radiology work of that bill stops and the accounts are reversed.

Register details, calculator, expense

  • Register details (briefcase icon): your sales, payments, refunds, credit sales, expenses, cash by method and tests sold. Print it on A4, A5 or the 80 mm printer; Close register is here too.
  • Calculator icon for quick sums.
  • Add Expense: money paid out of your drawer (see Expenses).

Patients

Reception › Patients

Everyone registered at the lab, with MRN, Name, Gender, Age, Phone and Last visit. Search by name, phone, MRN or CNIC. Add registers a patient, the edit icon changes details, and the New Bill icon opens the POS with that patient already chosen. Patients can't be deleted.

Token queue and home collection

Reception › Token Queue · Reception › Home Collection

Token queue

Issue token: choose the Counter (Reception, Sampling, Report collection, Cash), the patient or a walk-in name, and tick Priority for elderly or emergency (token starts with P-). Then use Call, Serving, Done or Skip. Status: Waiting → Called → Serving → Done.

Home collection

Book collection: patient, date & time, phone, address, tests requested, rider and charges. Status moves Booked → Assigned → On the way → Collected → Received at lab (or Cancelled with a reason). After that the sample follows the normal lab steps.

Lab worklist and sample collection

Laboratory › Lab Worklist (also Sample Collection, Result Entry, Verification, Lab Reports)

One screen with four tabs: To collect, Enter results, To verify and Done. Each lab menu item opens it on its tab. Each row shows the Lab #, the patient, progress (e.g. "0/2 verified") and the date.

  1. On To collect, find the patient (search or scan) and click Collect samples. The sample barcodes are shown and the tube labels print by themselves. Stick them on the tubes.
  2. Print labels prints again (and collects any sample not yet collected).
  3. The order moves to Enter results. Results can't be typed before the sample is collected.

Entering and verifying results

Lab Worklist › Open
  1. Click Open on an order. You see the patient, the sample barcodes and one card per test.
  2. Type each Result. The normal range for the patient's gender and age is shown, and the Flag fills in by itself: Normal, Low, High, Critical low, Critical high or Abnormal. Calculated values (marked "auto") fill in on their own.
  3. Add Comments if needed and click Save. The test becomes Entered.
  4. The pathologist checks it and clicks Verify. Only verified tests print or are shared.
Changing a result that is already verified needs special permission and a Reason for changing a verified result. It is recorded.

Report entry and printing

Laboratory › Report Entry
  1. Type the invoice number in Enter invoice no.. The tests of that bill are listed with tick boxes.
  2. Enter or check results as above. If the sample isn't collected yet, use Collect samples here.
  3. Print: Print prints the ticked tests, Auto Print prints all verified tests, and Continuous Print prints without a new page for each department.

Sharing a report with the patient

Lab Worklist › Share, or Report Entry › Share
  1. Click Share. Choose how long the link works: 1, 3, 7 or 30 days. Click Create link.
  2. Copy the link, Send on WhatsApp, or use SMS text. Only verified tests are in it.
  3. The list shows each link and how many times it was opened. Stop link stops it at once.

Critical results and outsourced tests

Laboratory › Critical Results · Laboratory › Outsourced Tests

Critical Results lists every dangerous value (time, patient, test, value, doctor, informed). Inform the doctor or patient at once. Open order goes to the results.

Outsourced Tests (tests sent to another lab): on Waiting to send, pick the reference lab, tick the tests, enter the courier and click Send to lab (the dispatch list prints). In Sent batches, mark them Received, attach their report and enter the result.

Tests, prices and normal ranges

Laboratory › Setup › Tests · Test Particulars

Every lab starts with a full list of tests with their parameters and normal ranges. The owner or lab in-charge adjusts it:

  • Tests: category, code, name, short name for labels, sample, tube, report type, price, report time, method. The row action Parameters & ranges opens its parameters.
  • Test Particulars: each value on the report with unit, result type (Number, Text, Pick list, Formula) and Male / Female / Child range. Ranges can be typed like 13 - 17, < 200 or Negative. Age-wise ranges sets ranges by age and critical limits.

Radiology worklist and scanning

Radiology › Radiology Worklist
Bill exam→Arrived→Start→Scan done→Report→Sign & verify→Print

Tabs: Waiting, Arrived / scanning, To report, Verified, Cancelled, All. Filter by date, modality and room.

  1. When the patient comes, click Arrived. (Book gives them a time slot instead.)
  2. In Arrived / scanning, click Start, do the scan, then Scan done: choose room / machine and technician, film size, films printed, films wasted and why, and notes for the radiologist. Click Mark scanned.
  3. The exam moves to To report for the radiologist.

Film Usage lists films used and wasted per exam.

Radiology schedule and appointments

Radiology › Schedule · Reception › Appointments

The schedule shows rooms and booked slots by day or week. Book appointment: choose the day, the room / machine and a free slot. Appointments can be marked Arrived, moved, or cancelled with a reason.

Radiology reporting (radiologist)

Radiology › Reporting
  1. Pick a patient from the To report queue on the left.
  2. Click Template to start from a ready report, then edit Findings, Impression and Recommendation. Tick Critical finding and note who was informed when needed.
  3. Save draft (Ctrl+S) any time. Sign & verify finalises it; after that it can only be changed with Amend and a reason.
  4. Print from here or from Radiology Reports.

Bills: payments and cancelling

Billing › Bills

Every bill with Total, Paid now, Due and status (Due, Partly paid, Paid, Refunded, Cancelled). Cancelled bills are shown crossed out.

  • Add payment (patient pays a balance): method, date, amount (it shows the most that can be paid) and reference.
  • View payments: every payment of the bill.
  • Cancel bill: reason required (at least 3 letters); if money was paid it shows the amount to give back and how. Cash refunds need your register open.

Receipts and Refunds are read-only lists of money in and out (today by default).

Shifts (close register) and cash book

Billing › Shifts (Open / Close) · Cash Book · Cash Transfers

Shifts lists every register session: opened, closed, counter, user, cash in hand, expected cash, cash counted and short / excess. Registers are opened from the POS; closing can be done from the POS or here (a supervisor can close a shift someone left open).

  1. Click Close register. Total Cash is filled in; enter card slips and cheques.
  2. Count the notes in Cash Denominations (count × note). The box shows Short, Excess or Balanced.
  3. Add a closing note and close. If cash does not match it asks to confirm.

Cash Book: every cash in / out with running balance. Cash Transfers: Hand over cash to another counter or make a Bank deposit; the receiver presses Receive (or Reject with a reason).

Expenses and day closing

Billing › Expenses · Billing › Day Closing

Expenses: money paid out (tea, courier, repairs). Choose Category, Date, Amount, Payment method, Paid to and details. Cash comes from your open register. Deleting asks for a reason.

Day closing (manager)

  1. Make sure every cashier has closed their register.
  2. Open Day Closing, pick the date and check the figures: sales, cash / card / online received, refunds, expenses, doctor payouts, dues, insurer credit and short / excess per counter.
  3. Write closing notes and click Close for that date. If registers are still open it warns you. A closed day can be printed on A4 or re-opened with a reason.

Referring doctors, commission and payouts

Billing › Referring Doctors · Commissions · Doctor Payouts

Add doctors with their commission rate. Every bill with a doctor adds a line to Commissions (Unpaid / Paid).

  1. New payout: choose the doctor and the bill dates, withholding tax and any adjustment, then Save draft.
  2. Pay: choose how (cash from your open register, bank, cheque, JazzCash, Easypaisa) and Mark paid.
  3. Slip prints a payout slip for the doctor to sign.

Insurance, panels and Sehat Card

Insurance
  1. Insurance Providers: the company, its rate list, discount, credit limit, payment days, co-payment and whether an authorization number is needed.
  2. Coverage: what it pays for (a whole lab department, one test, a radiology modality or one exam). Nothing listed = covers everything.
  3. Insurance Cards: the patient's card. Cards typed in the POS are saved here by themselves.
  4. Bill the patient in New Bill with Paid by = the insurer. The bill appears in Insured Bills.
  5. Claims › New claim: provider and date range. When money arrives, Receive payment (amount, cheque no., tax withheld). Status: Sent → Partial → Paid.

Lab Inventory (reagents, kits, tubes, films)

Lab Inventory
Purchase Order→Receive goods (GRN)→Pay supplier→Use / count
  1. Items and Suppliers: set them up once.
  2. Purchase Orders › New purchase order: supplier, dates, items. Save draft or Save as ordered. From the order, Receive goods.
  3. Purchases › Receive goods (GRN): supplier bill no., items with lot and expiry, discount, freight, and anything paid now. Stock goes up and the supplier balance grows.
  4. Supplier Payments › Pay supplier: amount, how, and which bills (oldest first or choose). Cash comes from your open register.
  5. Purchase Returns: send back goods by lot with a reason.

Stock and stock count

Stock shows on hand, average cost, value, reorder level and nearest expiry, with badges Low, Out, Near expiry and Expired lot. Consumption lists every stock movement.

  1. Stock Count › Start count: date and items. Print sheet to count on paper.
  2. Enter the Counted amounts and Save count. Only differences shows what doesn't match.
  3. Post adjustments (with a reason) corrects the stock.

Accounts

Accounts

Bills, payments, refunds, expenses, purchases, supplier payments and payroll go into the accounts by themselves. You only enter other transactions.

  1. Fiscal Years: create the current year first. A banner reminds you, with a button to create it (1 July – 30 June).
  2. Journal Entries › New voucher: date, type (Journal, Payment, Receipt, Opening balance), narration, then lines of Account / Debit / Credit. It saves only when Balanced (debit = credit).
  3. Ledger: choose an account and dates and click Run. Post billing to ledger sends any missing billing entries (safe to repeat).
  4. Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet for any date, with CSV and Print.

Reports

Reports

Sales, Collections, Test-wise, Radiology-wise, Doctor-wise, Branch-wise, Discounts, Turnaround Time and Profit per Test. Choose a period (Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, This month or your own dates) and branch, click Show, then Excel (CSV) or Print.

HR dashboard

HR & Payroll › HR Dashboard

Staff numbers, who is on leave, contracts due, requests waiting and the payroll of the month at a glance.

Employees: adding staff

HR & Payroll › Employees

The Employee List shows cards for Total, Active, Inactive, Outsourced, On Probation, On Notice and On Leave, and the staff table.

  1. Click Add Employee. The form has 11 sections on the left; sections marked Required must be filled.
  2. Name: title, first / middle / last name, relation and father / husband name.
  3. Employment Information: employee no., date of joining, department, section, position, contract.
  4. CNIC & Documents, Personal Information (gender, date of birth…), Contact & Access (mobile, email, app login).
  5. Salary & Payment: basic salary, salary type and payment method (bank details). EOBI & Income Tax: EOBI number and tax settings.
  6. Reporting (direct manager), Additional, Upload and Approval Overrides, then click Add Employee.
If employee numbers are set to Sequential in HR Settings › Preferences, the next number fills in by itself.

Employee profile

Employees › click a name

The profile has tabs: General, Employment, Compensation, Attendance, Leaves, Requests, Records (documents, assets), Performance, Transfer, End of Service, Roles and Dependents.

Requests and approvals

HR & Payroll › Requests

Staff requests: Leave, Loan, Salary advance, Overtime, Short leave permit, Letter, Missing punch and Other.

  1. Click Add Request. Step 1 Select Type, step 2 Fill Details, step 3 Review & Submit.
  2. The request goes to its approvers in order. Each approver opens it and clicks Approve (comments optional) or Reject (reason required).
  3. Status: Pending → Approved / Rejected (or Cancelled).
Who approves, and in what order, is set in HR Settings › Request Settings › Approval Sequence (one after another, or all at the same time). With no approvers set, HR approves.

Attendance

HR & Payroll › Attendance Records
  1. Choose the month.
  2. Download template file, fill it from your attendance machine, then Import attendance (.csv).
  3. Click Calculate. The cards show absences, late arrivals, overtime and missed check-outs with their amounts.
  4. On the Attendance Summary tab tick the rows and click Post to payroll. (The month's payroll must be generated first: Payroll step 1.) Unpost undoes it.

Day status can be Present, Late, Absent, Half Day, Leave, Holiday, Day off or Missed Check-out. If Mark absent automatically is on, working days with no attendance are marked absent each night.

Shift schedule

HR & Payroll › Shift Schedule

A weekly grid of who works which shift. Click an empty day (or Assign Shift) to give a shift; click a shift to change it. Quick shift, Print and Shift history are at the top.

Leaves and the leave planner

HR & Payroll › Leaves · Leave Planner
  1. New Request: employee, leave type, start and end date, reason → Submit.
  2. The approver clicks Approve or Reject, adds remarks if wanted, and confirms.
  3. Leave Planner shows who is off when, by day, week or month; Export saves it.

Loans and advances

HR & Payroll › Loans

Add Loan / Advance: employee, amount, monthly installment, loan date and the month recovery starts. The installment is cut from each salary when payroll is posted. Open a loan to see its Payment Schedule, edit the installment, or print. Status: Active → Fully Repaid (or Terminated).

Payroll: the 5 steps

HR & Payroll › Payroll
1 Select Period→2 Process Attendance→3 Review Payroll→4 Approval→5 Posting

Payroll History (top right) shows past months.

Step 1: Select Period

The month is shown with its dates. Tick the departments and sections to include (Select All / Deselect All) and generate the payroll to continue.

Step 2: Process Attendance

Check each person's attendance days, absences, late minutes and overtime. Click Calculate, then post the summaries to the payroll and continue.

Step 3: Review Payroll

Totals at the top (employees, entitlements, deductions, net salary) and every employee's basic, allowances, deductions and net. Open a Payslip to check one person. Then click Finish & Submit for Approval.

Step 4: Approval

Preview the payroll summary, download Excel, print payslips, add a note for the approver and click Submit for Approval. Each approver clicks Approve Step or Reject (with a reason); after a rejection, fix it and resubmit. With no payroll approvers set, the owner (or a user allowed to approve payroll) approves in one step.

Step 5: Posting

Pay the approved payroll from a Cash register or a Bank account: choose where it's paid from, the cheque / transfer reference and the date, then post. Loan installments are recovered, the salary voucher (with EOBI) goes to the accounts, and the month is closed.

  • Employee numbers: sequential (with a format such as EMP-{0000}) or typed by hand.
  • Contract expiry alert days for the HR dashboard; Show outsourced staff.
  • Mark absent automatically and Return to work automatically (run each night).
  • Allow back-dated requests.

First-time setup (owner)

Do these once, in this order, before the lab starts billing.

  1. Settings › Lab Profile: name, phone, address, NTN, licence no., logo, header and footer text.
  2. Settings › Report Layout and Signatures: letterhead, paper size, and the pathologist's / radiologist's signature.
  3. Settings › Branches and Billing › Cash Registers (one per counter).
  4. Settings › Users: one login per person, each with a role.
  5. Laboratory › Setup › Tests: check prices.
  6. Billing › Referring Doctors with commission rates.
  7. Accounts › Fiscal Years: create the current year.
  8. Insurance › Insurance Providers: Sehat Card and your panels.
